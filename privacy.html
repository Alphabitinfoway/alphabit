Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

Alphabit Infoway (“Alphabit Infoway,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting information provided through your use of our website, https://alphabitinfoway.com/ (the “Website”).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you access or use the Website and interact with its forms, content, and third-party technologies.

1. Contact Form

The Website provides contact forms that allow you to contact Alphabit Infoway regarding our services, projects, consultations, employment opportunities, or other inquiries.

Through the contact form, we may collect:

  • First name
  • Last name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Subject
  • Message

Information submitted through the contact form is transmitted to our website backend and may be used to receive, review, and respond to your inquiry.

The Website also provides a career application form. If you apply for an employment opportunity through the Website, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Resume/CV

Information submitted through the career application form may be used for recruitment, candidate evaluation, and employment-related communication.

You should not submit passwords, payment-card information, or other sensitive personal information through the Website's contact or career forms.

2. Google Tag Manager

The Website uses Google Tag Manager (GTM) with container ID GTM-N7XK5D9C.

Google Tag Manager is a tag-management system that allows website tags and related technologies to be managed and deployed on the Website.

Depending on the tags configured within the Google Tag Manager container, information such as browser type, device information, page views, and website interactions may be processed by the technologies deployed through the container.

The information collected through these technologies depends on the specific tags and services configured on the Website.

3. Cookies / Tracking Technologies

The Website may use cookies and similar technologies through Google Tag Manager and other website technologies.

These technologies may be used to support Website functionality, understand Website usage, and enable technologies configured through the Website's tag-management system.

The specific cookies or tracking technologies used may vary depending on the third-party tags and services configured on the Website.

You may manage or restrict cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies or similar technologies may affect the availability or functionality of certain Website features.

4. Third-Party Services

The Website may use or load certain third-party services and resources, including:

  • Google Tag Manager
  • Google Maps
  • Google Fonts
  • Cloudinary
  • cdnjs
  • Social media services linked from the Website

These third-party services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms of service.

For example, the Contact page includes a Google Maps integration, and certain Website images or other assets may be delivered through Cloudinary.

Alphabit Infoway does not control the privacy practices of third-party services. You should review the applicable privacy policies and terms of service of those third parties when using or interacting with their services.

5. External Links

The Website may contain links to external websites and social media platforms, including LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, and X.

When you access an external website through a link on the Website, you leave the Alphabit Infoway Website and become subject to the privacy practices and terms applicable to that external website.

Alphabit Infoway does not control and is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content, or policies of external websites.

You should review the privacy policy and terms of service of any external website you access.

6. Data Security

Alphabit Infoway takes reasonable measures designed to protect information submitted through the Website against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

The Website is served through HTTPS when accessed through https://alphabitinfoway.com/.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure. Accordingly, while we take reasonable measures to protect information, we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted to or through the Website.

7. Data Retention

Alphabit Infoway retains information submitted through the Website only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.

This may include:

  • Responding to inquiries
  • Communicating with prospective clients
  • Processing recruitment applications
  • Maintaining appropriate business records
  • Complying with applicable legal or regulatory requirements

The applicable retention period may vary depending on the nature of the information, the purpose for which it was collected, and applicable legal or business requirements.

Where the Website or our business processes do not establish a specific retention period, information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.
